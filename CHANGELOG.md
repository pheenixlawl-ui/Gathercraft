## v0.2.0 – 2026-11-20

### Added
- **Farm for plan – the skill plan's shopping list on the map:** the skill plan has a new button "Farm for plan". It starts a farm run in the zone you are in for the materials your plan still needs that come from herb or ore nodes. Their nodes glow on the map and minimap, the other pins are dimmed. The run window counts what you have against what the plan needs and lists what is "Not on the map"; gather or buy something and the count follows. When everything from the map is there, the run ends by itself. A material of a gathering profession you don't have says "needs Herbalism" or "needs Mining" – the run starts anyway, so you can see where it grows. Nothing is promised about time or amount: known spawn points are possible spawns, not every one is up.
- **Where to farm:** a second button lists the zones of your continent with known spots for the missing materials, with the number of spots for each material. Your own zone is marked "(here)"; "Go" starts the plan run in the zone of that row.
- **Item tooltips for herbs, ores and reagents:** a Gathercraft block with the auction price – what kind of price it is (own scan or lowest offer), how old it is and, when that is known, how many were listed – and the last 14 days (low, usual, high). Hold Shift for the last 7 days, the zone with the most known spawn points (Questie data and your own finds are named) and the professions that use the item. What is not known says "unknown". To turn it off: Options › Rares & prices › "Gathercraft info in item tooltips".
- **Trainers and recipe vendors with a place:** under "Train the next rank" the skill plan names the nearest trainer ("Nearest trainer: …"), and a recipe sold by a vendor names the nearest vendor; a click sets a waypoint. A trainer whose title does not name a rank gets the note "May not teach this rank." The places come from the Questie database (QuestieDB), used with the Questie team's permission – see NOTICE.md. They are places from data: the NPC may not be there right now, and trainers in the new Forever zones are not in the data.
- **`/gcr detest`:** a new debug command for testing disenchant data – it only prints to chat while it is switched on, does nothing in the game and saves nothing.

### Changed
- **Skill plan, rebuilt:** one calculation for every profession. Pick a profession on the Crafting tab and click "Skill plan"; the target comes from a menu and stays as you set it. Materials are counted once, in the order the plan is carried out, and making an intermediate yourself is compared with buying it.
- **Skill plan, what you really have:** "have / need" for every material, from bags, reagent bag and bank. A row says "enough" only when your own stock covers the need; what the plan's own steps will make is called a forecast.
- **Skill plan, honest numbers:** each step says the chance for a skill point per craft and about how many crafts it takes, with a range ("in 8 of 10 cases between …") – a range of the model, not a guarantee. A recipe without colour data says "chance unknown" and is not counted. Prices are known, estimated or unknown: nothing stands in for a missing price, and what has no price is not in the sum – the plan says how many that are. The recipes are chosen by the gold to spend on top of what you have: a sensible plan, not a proven least costly one.
- **Skill plan, your skill:** it is read from the game; training the next rank is a step of its own, and recipes to learn are listed with their trainer fee or their vendor. For Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering and Tailoring Gathercraft goes by the game's usual profession numbers, which we have not seen in the Forever client yet. Where the plan shows "Your skill: ?", open the profession window once.
- **The auction price line in item tooltips** says what kind of price it is and how old; how many were listed only when that is known.

### Fixed
- **A saved farm run goes on only for the character that started it.** Before, another character of your account could take it up or end it at login – normal farm runs too, since 0.1.x. One-time: a run saved before 0.2 is not continued; start it again.
