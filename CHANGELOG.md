## v0.1.23 – 2026-11-16

### Faster
- **Minimap pins only near you:** every node of your zone had a minimap pin, and the map library places every one of them again whenever you move – also the ones half a zone away. Sven measured it in Stranglethorn while walking: Gathercraft took up to 20 % of the addon time, about 5 % with "Show nodes on the minimap" off. Now only nodes within 400 yards have a minimap pin, and a pin goes when its node is more than 450 yards away. Gathercraft looks again once you are 50 yards on, or after 2 seconds when you moved at all. You see the same as before: the minimap shows about 233 yards at its widest zoom outdoors, about 330 into the corners of a square one. World map pins, questing rings, the run target and the route line are unchanged. In our test (Stranglethorn's known spawn points plus 300 nodes of our own, not the live game) 32 of 1,234 pins were left where we stood. `/gcr perf` now ends with "minimap pins: N near / M in zone". How much it is in the game we have not measured yet.
- **Sharing, nodes that are sent again:** when a message names a node with the same kind, place and item as one Gathercraft has seen before, it is no longer taken apart and searched by distance – it goes straight to the node or the waiting report it led to last time. What happens then is the same as before. In our test (not the live game) a minute of answers about nodes you already know took about 60 % less time in the handlers; a minute of answers you hear for the first time a little less (5–20 %), because most of that time is taking the new nodes in; single harvests the same. `/gcr perf` tells what it is in the game.
- **Auction house scan in small steps:** reading a large scan and working out its prices is now split into small steps by time, not by a number of auctions or items – also when most auctions are of a few items. Before, all prices were worked out in one go at the end. In our tests with 300,000 auctions (our test machine, not the game) the steps stayed short however the auctions were spread over the items. How it feels in the game we have not measured yet. A scan whose auctions are all read is finished even when you close the window.

### Added
- **The auction house scan starts when you open the window.** Opening the auction house starts the scan, at most once in the 15 minutes the server allows. Chat stays quiet when it cannot start, gets no answer (another addon may have used up the server's scan) or the window closes; the button shows what is going on, and the result is said as before. To turn it off: Options › Rares & prices › "Scan the auction house automatically when I open it". The button and `/gcr scan` work as before.
- **Options › Fishing:** the four fishing switches have a page of their own, each with a line that says what it does. Your settings are kept.
- **A switch for the ping:** Options › General › "Ping when I reach a route point". On by default; off, the run goes on as before without the sound.
- **The skill plan reads your profession skill from the game itself.** Gathercraft now reads your professions from the game's own list by their number, and every place of the list (the Forever client has seven; only six were read). Professions are now detected by their ID, so the German 'Alchimie' name should no longer matter – please report if it still does. In the Forever client we have seen this for Herbalism, Mining, First Aid, Fishing and Cooking; for the crafting professions Gathercraft uses the game's usual numbers, which we have not seen there yet. Where the plan still shows "Your skill: ?", open the profession window once, as before. `/gcr crafttest` lists what the game says: place, name, skill and number.
