## v0.1.21 – 2026-11-12

### Fixed
- **Questing mode, a gold ring on a vein that is gone:** the starter data holds one entry for every sort a spot can grow – Copper, Tin and Silver Vein at the same spot are three entries. After you mined the tin, the other two still got a ring. Entries of one kind (herb, ore, chest) within 10 yards now count as one spot: it gets one ring, and none while any entry there rests (just harvested, found empty, or parked). Nothing in your recorded nodes changes.
- **Questing mode, short hitches while walking:** every look went through all nodes of the zone and read your skill for each of them. Now your skill is read once per look, and only the nodes within the radius are asked whether they rest. In Hillsbrad Foothills (668 spawn points) one look read the skill 650 times before and once now; measured outside the game a look takes about 0.03 ms instead of 0.8 ms.
- **Minimap tooltip with a UI that covers the minimap:** with Ellesmere UI the game's tooltip named the node, but Gathercraft's line below it and the learning by pointing did nothing – a frame of the UI, as large as the minimap, lies over it. A frame that is a direct child of the minimap and has its size and place (within 4 pixels) now counts as the minimap. Pins of other addons still do not.
- **"Fish key gathers the marked node" blocked fishing in the questing mode:** the key was "interact" for as long as the questing mode was on, so it could not cast. In the questing mode it is now your Fish key; it switches to gathering only while the game reports a herb, a vein or a chest as marked (an object from the starter data, or one with a known entry in the zone you are in – your own or a shared one). During a farm run the key gathers as before.
- **Herbs/minerals button invisible with Ellesmere UI:** the button was shown but fully transparent, as a child of the minimap. It is now a child of the screen, anchored at the minimap and hidden with it. 0.1.20 said the button sits on the edge of a square minimap: that only holds for UIs that tell their shape (GetMinimapShape), and Ellesmere UI does not. For such UIs there is a new choice: Options › General › Minimap shape (Auto / Round / Square).
- **Craft log, Create All ended by moving:** the cast you interrupt was counted as a failure inside the series, and all its clean crafts landed under "overlapping". A failed or interrupted cast at the end now leaves the series: the block is the crafts before it. A failure with more crafts of the same series after it leaves everything overlapping, as before.
- **Craft log, a false "gave more than one point":** switching the recording off and on during Create All (also `/gcr craftlog clear`, a reload, or a craft with a hidden cast ID) could make the next craft look like two points, and the series of that profession were no longer counted. A craft that starts within 1.5 s after a craft the log did not read, or whose start was not seen at all, is now marked overlapping: it is in no rate and proves nothing. The same holds after a spell success the game hides the identity of – it may have been a craft; nothing is logged for it, and a craft being cast at that moment is marked the same way. A note that was set by this error stays until you run `/gcr craftlog clear` – an old entry does not tell whether it was real.

### Changed
- **Questing mode, minimap style "Ring only":** the rings are the thin gold ring now, at the same size. The next node of a farm run keeps the broad ring.
- **Craft log, header:** "crafts" counts finished crafts only. Failed and interrupted casts stand under "Not counted".
- **Sharing, the chat line "… spots shared by other players added":** it is off by default now, also if you never touched the option. To get it back: Options › Sharing › "Tell me in chat when spots of other players arrive". Sharing itself is unchanged.
