## v0.2.1 – 2026-11-21

### Changed
- **Shipped spots stay on your map:** the herb and ore spots for Riverglades, Mount Hyjal and Zephras Isle no longer disappear after 21 days. The 0.2.0 notes said unconfirmed spots would leave – that clock also ran in zones you had never visited, so the first would have gone on 11 December. Now they stay until you harvest them, including those 0.2.0 already added.
- **"Starter data", not "beta":** shipped spots now say "Gathercraft starter data (not harvested by you yet)" in their tooltip, and the chat line when new spots are added starts with "Starter data".
- **Quest objects are not recorded as herbs or ore:** some quest objects in Riverglades show up in the herb and ore search. If an unknown object gives only quest items, Gathercraft no longer records or shares it. New herbs and veins are recorded as before, and so is any gather whose loot can't be fully read.
- **Windows stay open over a loading screen:** after a hearthstone, portal or instance, the game closed Gathercraft's windows. Now a window that was open comes back once after the loading screen; one you close stays closed.

### Fixed
- **Minimap tooltip below the game's:** Gathercraft's minimap tooltip now sits below the game's tooltip instead of on top of it – also when the game adds quest lines to it.
